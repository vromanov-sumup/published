Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

myproject is a privately operated application used by its owner to access and manage personal scheduling information.

Information accessed

When authorized, myproject can access Google Calendar information, which may include calendar identifiers, event titles, descriptions, dates, times, locations, and attendees.

How information is used

Calendar information is used to respond to the owner's requests about their schedule and, when requested, create, modify, or delete calendar events. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used for marketing profiles.

Storage and security

Authorization credentials and application state are stored in an access-controlled, privately managed environment. Related information may be retained in operational logs or backups depending on the owner's configuration.

External services

To carry out requests, relevant information may be shared with configured AI or messaging service providers. Those services handle information under their own privacy terms. Calendar information is not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access through Google Account permissions and remove stored credentials or related records from the application and its backups as applicable.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support contact listed on the application's Google authorization screen.
